Support

Jaimes Field Tools is designed for surveying and construction workflows including level loops, point creation, total station setup records, foundation checkout, PDF/DXF tools, calculations, surface tools, and field reports.

Common Help

  • No account or login is required.
  • The app is designed to work offline for normal field use.
  • Photos and imported files are used only for app features you choose to use.
  • Reports can be generated and exported from supported modules.

Privacy

Jaimes Field Tools does not require user registration. Data entered into the app is intended for local field workflows. The app may request access to the camera or photo library only when you choose features that use photos or exported images.
