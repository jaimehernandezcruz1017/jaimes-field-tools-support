Privacy Policy • Effective October 5, 2026

Privacy Overview

Jaimes Field Tools is designed as an offline surveying and construction field utility. The app does not require an account or login and does not collect personal data from users.

Data Storage

Survey records, calculations, imported files, photos, drawings, and reports are intended to remain on the user's device unless the user chooses to export, share, or save them elsewhere.

Camera and Photo Library

The app may request access to the camera or photo library only when the user chooses features that attach, import, save, or export photos and images. Jaimes Field Tools does not use these permissions to collect or transmit user data to the developer.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Jaimes Field Tools does not use advertising, third-party tracking, or analytics services to collect user data.

Third-Party Sharing

Jaimes Field Tools does not sell user information and does not share user data with advertisers or data brokers.
